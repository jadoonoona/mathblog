## Interested in adopting a rescued Jindo or Jindo mix from Korea?

Please check out these websites. They are a starting point for meeting dogs looking for a home and learning how to apply.

- **[Rebel Rescue South Korea — available dogs](https://rebelrescuesk.com/available-dogs/)**: Meet rescue dogs, including Jindo mixes. Its [adoption process](https://rebelrescuesk.com/adoption-process/) covers applications from families in Korea and selected overseas destinations.
- **[Jindo Love Rescue](https://jindoloverescue.org/)**: Rescues dogs in South Korea and places them in homes in the United States. Read its [adoption FAQ](https://jindoloverescue.org/faq/) for the process and how to get in touch.
- **[Korean K9 Rescue — adoptable dogs](https://www.koreank9rescue.org/adopt/)**: Browse dogs rescued from Korea, including Jindo mixes, and follow the links to their profiles and applications. Check its [adoption FAQ](https://www.koreank9rescue.org/adoption-faq/) for eligible adoption areas in the United States.

Start with the organization that serves your location, read the dog's profile, and contact the rescue about a suitable match. Available dogs, adoption areas and application requirements can change; each organization's website has the latest details.
